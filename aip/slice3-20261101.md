# RNAV SID/STAR slice 3 extract (AIRAC 1 Nov 2026)

Derived from AIP Japan (AIRAC 1 Nov 2026), may not be current, edited by the Horus project and not government-made.

Companion file: `slice3-20261101.json` (same procedure/waypoint/leg schema as `rjtt-sid-star-20261101.json`). This file is **additive** to the slice-1 catalog; it does not replace VAMOS FOUR or the RJTT STAR 15 already coded there, and it does not touch the slice-2 departure-sample work.

Source plates are the 22 RNAV PDFs in `aip/20261101/` (`JP-AD-2.24.13–18,87-RJTT` and `JP-AD-2.24.10–14,16,18,25,26,30,45–49-RJAA`). Text was taken with `pdftotext -layout`. Coordinates, path terminators, and altitude/speed constraints were not invented. A published value that could not be read was left out of that field, or the procedure would have been excluded; none of the 22 plates required that exclusion.

## Included

IDs follow the slice-1 pattern: SID `FAMILY#`.`RWY…` (combined `RWY34L_34R` when the table is headed `RWY34L/RWY34R`), transition `FAMILY#`.`NAME`, STAR `FAMILY` plus the published letter (`RUTASE`, `RUTAST`, `RUTASG`, `RUTASN`, and the same for SWAMP / SUPOK / LUBLA).

Letter suffixes on TIARA / BEKLA / ROVER, and E/T versus G/N on the RJAA STARs, are **not** mapped to a single runway. Applicable runways are those printed on the coding-table header (STAR chart headers are `RNAV STAR RWY34L/34R` for E/T and `RWY16R/16L` for G/N). Variants that the plate does not publish for a runway (for example TIARA THREE B/C, BEKLA FOUR B / FIVE C, and ROVER FOUR B/C have no 34L, 04, or 22 table) are omitted, not invented.

## Excluded, and why

### From these 22 plates

**None.** Every RNAV SID runway variant, published transition, and STAR on the 22 plates has a readable coding table and a complete waypoint list with lat/lon. No path terminator on these plates is outside `{IF, TF, VA, DF, Hold}`. No coordinate was guessed.

Some chart/narrative pages (notably GUSRO introductory text) use a symbol font that `pdftotext` garbles. Coding tables and coordinate tables on those plates are plain and were used instead. Climb-gradient notes and DME-gap remarks were not turned into constraints.

CHANGE footers that split a coding row across a page break (for example ROVER THREE A RWY16L serial 004, OLVAN TWO RWY16L serial 003/004) were reassembled from the printed fragments, not filled in from the magnetic model.

### Not in this fetch (out of slice 3, not “unreadable on these plates”)

These remain excluded because they were not among the 22 RNAV plates, or were already known uncodable in slices 1–2:

- VISIP ONE (RJTT conventional SID): no coding table, no VISIP lat/lon, TTE absent from Horus navaid/waypoint catalogs.
- Conventional SID/STAR plates still listed in `spec-draft-16-sid-star-slice2.md` and not in this 22-file set (SEKIYADO, OPPAR, ISOGO, SAKURA, AKAGI SID, SUNNS, SULPU/OLRAT, SOUTH/NORTH, and the other remaining official `JP-AD-2.24.xx` IDs).
- Slice-1 RJTT STARs (OSHIMA, AKSEL, AROSA, POLIX, GODIN, …) and VAMOS FOUR: already coded; not duplicated here.
- Instrument approach procedures, missed-approach, and holding patterns as automatically flown legs.

## Geometry check

For every TF leg whose both ends have published coordinates and a published distance and true course, a spherical great-circle (radius 6371 km, 1 NM = 1852 m) was compared with the table. Sixteen pairs exceed the slice-1 flag of 0.05 NM or 0.05°. The largest difference is BORLO TWO RWY34L/34R CUPID–BORLO (0.069 NM, 0.073°). Recurring 0.051° flags on T6L23–WELDA, and similar 0.05–0.06 values on several RJAA STAR legs, are consistent with sphere-versus-published rounding. **Coordinates and published courses were not changed.** These procedures stay included.

## Field conventions (same as slice 1)

- Bare altitude = AT; prefix `+` = at or above; prefix `-` = at or below; `FLnnn` stored as a flight level.
- Magnetic and true courses are both kept as printed. Horus headings are a geodetic/true frame; do not apply the printed variation to simulator headings.
- `sourceNotice` is the one source line above. There is no UI warning banner in this extract.
- Package `airports` lists RJTT and RJAA. Each procedure still has `airportIcao`.
- Each procedure has `sourcePages` naming the AIP page identifier and the package PDF page.

Page-footer effective dates on several plates are older than the package AIRAC date (the plate was not reissued). That is recorded under `uncertain` and on each chart record; it is not treated as a reason to exclude.
